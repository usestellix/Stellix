### Description

```
Stellix is a non-custodial wallet for the Stellar network. This release works on the Stellar TESTNET only: test XLM has no value, so it's a safe place to try Stellar payments.

YOUR KEYS STAY YOURS
• Create a wallet with a new 12-word recovery phrase, or import an existing one (12 or 24 words; same accounts as other SEP-0005 wallets).
• The phrase is encrypted with your password and never leaves your device. Stellix Labs runs no servers and can't see, recover or move your funds.
• Auto-lock after 5 to 60 minutes.

SEND AND RECEIVE XLM
• See your total, available and reserved balance.
• Send XLM to any G… or M… address. The review screen shows exactly what will be signed, including the network fee.
• Memos are required automatically for exchanges that ask for them (SEP-29).
• Receive with a QR code, or create a payment link for a specific amount (SEP-7).
• Activity history updates live while the wallet is open.
• Fund testnet accounts for free with Friendbot.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no tracking, no ads. To read balances and send payments, Stellix connects directly to a public Stellar Horizon server (by default, the Stellar Development Foundation's). That server can see your IP address and the account addresses you use. You can choose another Horizon server in Settings.

IMPORTANT
Write down your recovery phrase and keep it offline. If you lose both your password and your recovery phrase, nobody can restore your wallet. Never share your phrase with anyone. Stellix asks for it only when you import a wallet.
```

Every claim above maps to shipped behaviour: TESTNET only (ADR 0008), SEP-0005 compatibility (M1, Freighter check), auto-lock range (settings), review from the built transaction (CLAUDE.md §6.6), SEP-29 / SEP-7 (M3/M4), live history (M4), Friendbot (M2), custom Horizon (M2).
